Football Experience

Bridging the gap between data and reality.

INFORM is an independent recruitment and data analysis consultancy delivering high-resolution intelligence to clubs, recruitment departments, agencies, and players.

Decision making in today's game demands more than subjective opinion or numbers on a spreadsheet. By blending extensive 'eyes-on' scouting, industry-leading data, and bespoke statistical modeling, INFORM provides unambiguous recruitment dossiers tailored to specific models.

From identifying talent across the Professional, Academy and National League Systems to delivering in-depth pre-match opposition preparation, our work is grounded in practical application, tactical rigor, and professional integrity.

Our Services

Bespoke packages available to meet your needs and give you a competitive edge

01

Player

Individual talent evaluations tailored to your specific tactical game model and positional requirements.

  • Basic: Single-match live or video scouting report with core trait grading.
  • Detailed: Multi-match contextual dossier with evidence and role breakdown.
  • Complex: Full recruitment profiling with statistical benchmarking, succession mapping, and tactical fit analysis.
02

Data

Advanced performance metrics transformed into clear, actionable intelligence and custom visualizations.

  • Basic: Standard percentile metrics and individual performance visualisations.
  • Detailed: Positional cohort comparisons, league benchmarking, and squad metric distributions.
  • Complex: Bespoke predictive filtering, customized recruitment models, and market replacement shortlists.
03

Opposition

Tactical pre-match preparation dossiers designed to identify opponent tendencies and tactical vulnerabilities.

  • Basic: Structural shape overview, standard starting XI trends, and key player alerts.
  • Detailed: Tactical breakdown identifying strengths, weaknesses alongside set-piece analysis.
  • Complex: Comprehensive tactical gameplan dossiers with visual patterns, pressing triggers, and exploit strategies.
