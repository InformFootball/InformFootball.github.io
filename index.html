Football Experience

Bridging the gap between data and reality.

INFORM is an independent recruitment and data analysis consultancy delivering high-resolution intelligence to clubs, recruitment departments, agencies, and players.

Decision making in today's game demands more than subjective opinion or numbers on a spreadsheet. By blending extensive 'eyes-on' scouting, industry-leading data, and bespoke statistical modeling, INFORM provides unambiguous recruitment dossiers tailored to specific models.

From identifying talent across the Professional, Academy and National League Systems to delivering in-depth pre-match opposition preparation, our work is grounded in practical application, tactical rigor, and professional integrity.
