INFORM is an independent football recruitment and tactical analysis consultancy delivering high-resolution intelligence to football clubs, recruitment departments, sports agencies, and professional players.

Modern recruitment demands more than subjective opinion or raw spreadsheet numbers. By blending extensive video tracking, live stadium scouting, Wyscout event data, and bespoke statistical modeling (pizza charts, radar percentiles, and normalized performance indices), INFORM provides unambiguous recruitment dossiers tailored to specific club game models.

From identifying undervalued talent across the National League System and EFL to delivering in-depth pre-match opposition preparation, our work is grounded in practical application, tactical rigor, and professional integrity.
